Minotaur Traits

Large monstrous humanoid
Ability Modifiers: +12 STR, +4 CON, -2 INT, -2 CHA
Extra Starting Hit Dice: A Minotaur gains 4 bonus Hit Dice (4d8 hit points). The Minotaur's Constitution Modifier applies to each Hit Die when determining hit points.
Base Speed: 30 feet
Natura Armor Bonus: Minotaurs have a thick hide and gain a +5 bonus to defense.
Attack Bonus: Minotaurs gain a +3 species bonus on attack rolls.
Fighting Space: As a large sized creature Minotaurs have a ten foot fighting space.
Low-Light Vision: Minotaurs can see twice as far as a human in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. They retain the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions.
Natura Weapon: Minotaurs have a set of horns that grant a goar attack that deals a 1d10 Piercing damage. Bonus Feat: Archaic Weapon Proficiency or Simple Weapon Proficiency
